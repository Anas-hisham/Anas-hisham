#### [Smart Telesales](https://github.com/Anas-hisham/Smart-Telesales)  
*One platform for the whole sales lead lifecycle, instead of 8–10 separate tools*  
`React · TypeScript · Redux Toolkit · Tailwind`  
- **The problem:** sales agents lose a large part of their day switching between tools for leads, calls, calendar and email. I designed one platform that covers the entire lead lifecycle.  
- **Managers see progress without asking:** Team Leaders track each agent's calls, emails, meetings and sales against a daily target; Operation Managers see every team under them; Analytics shows the full funnel (leads → contacted → reached → meetings → sold), answer and reach rates, and best calling hours.  
- **Business rules live in code, not in memory:** automated call & email cadences, a 15-day rule that frees stale leads, and fair-play lead pool rules.  
- **Call comments become data:** agents' notes are classified into reasons (interested, price, timing, wrong contact...), with a data-quality check that warns managers when numbers are unreliable.  
- **API-first:** the full backend contract (**79 endpoints**, roles, error codes, idempotency, rate limiting, scheduled jobs) was documented before building. The front end already runs against it; the backend is the next phase.
